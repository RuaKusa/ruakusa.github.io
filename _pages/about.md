I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Illinois Chicago, where I work in the SPEAR Lab on machine learning and reinforcement learning methods for high-performance computing systems.

Research
======
My current work focuses on runtime prediction, uncertainty-aware buffering, and energy-aware optimization for HPC scheduling. I build predictive models from scheduler and system logs, study reinforcement learning policies for system-level decision making, and evaluate how these methods affect throughput, reliability, and energy use on large-scale clusters.

Research Interests
======
* High-performance computing systems
* Batch job runtime prediction
* Reinforcement learning for systems optimization
* Energy-aware scheduling
* Trustworthy machine learning for systems
